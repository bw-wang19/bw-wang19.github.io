Research

Research Interests

01

Model Merging & Distillation

Integrating complementary capabilities from specialized models through parameter-space merging and multi-teacher on-policy distillation (MOPD).

02

Post-Training & Agentic RL

Improving reasoning, tool use, and long-horizon decision-making through post-training and reinforcement learning in interactive environments.

03

Recursive Self-Improvement of Agents

Exploring recursive self-improvement (RSI), where agents iteratively refine their own code and workflows to improve subsequent rounds of learning and problem-solving.
